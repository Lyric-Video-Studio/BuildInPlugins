Lip sync (supplied audio) - Sync v3 Avatar (sync-lipsync/v3/image-to-video): animate a still face or illustrated character to an existing speech or singing audio file. Inputs: image + audio. This plugin exposes the image-to-video endpoint, not Sync v3 video-to-video.
Lip sync (supplied audio) - H3 Max Lip Sync (minimax/h3-max/lip-sync/image-to-video): animate a portrait or illustration to an existing speech or singing recording. Inputs: image + audio; optional transcription guidance (disable for songs/music).
Dialogue generation (native generated audio) - Wan 3.0 text-to-video and image-to-video (alibaba/wan-3.0): generate a talking or singing character with audio and visuals together, including synchronized spoken dialogue.
Audio-guided character performance - Wan 3.0 reference-to-video (alibaba/wan-3.0/reference-to-video): guide generated video using images, video and supplied reference audio for character performance and dialogue synchronization.
Generated dialogue - Veo 3.1 video generation: create characters speaking with generated audio and a dialogue prompt.
Generated dialogue - Kling 3 video generation: generate spoken scenes with in-model synchronized speech and audio.
Dialogue and audio reference - Seedance 2 reference-to-video: create video from images, video and audio references, including character performances.
Character animation - Scail 2 and Sync v3 Avatar animate a character/face; Sync v3 is the supplied-audio talking-avatar option.
Mirelo SFX 2.0 (video-to-audio) - Make audio based on a video, generate sound effects for footage, and replace audio from a video with a new synchronized soundtrack. Use it to add sound to silent AI-generated video, replace a video's original audio or soundtrack, produce Foley sound design and ambient noise matched to visible action, or generate audio/sound effects from video. Provide a video file or URL, optionally guide sounds with a text prompt, exclude unwanted sounds, and preserve or separately extract speech. Returns standalone audio files to place under the video in the timeline, not a muxed video file.
